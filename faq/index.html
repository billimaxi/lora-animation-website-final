GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we create.

What services does LORA offer?

AI animation, launch visuals, promotional videos, brand storytelling, music-video visuals and cultural animation. Voiceovers and supporting social content can be discussed in your brief. Explore services →

Which subscription should I choose?

Essential is $150 USD/month for 4 AI videos per month. Growth is $250 USD/month for 3 per week, up to 12 per month. Studio Retainer is $500 USD/month for 4 per week, up to 16 per month. Length, revisions, carousels and production schedules are agreed with LORA before work begins. Compare plans →

How do payments and billing work?

Each Subscribe button opens its plan's Flutterwave checkout. Review the amount, currency, recurring billing details and terms before authorizing payment. Payment details are entered on Flutterwave, not in this site's inquiry form. For payment issues, contact LORA with your transaction reference; never send card details.

Can I inquire about a one-off project before paying?

Yes. Use the project inquiry form, email or WhatsApp to share your goals, budget and timeline. An inquiry does not start a subscription or charge you. LORA will discuss scope and next steps with you.

How do I cancel, change a plan or ask about a refund?

Contact LORA at info@loraanimations.online with your plan and payment reference. Ask about cancellation timing, future billing and any work already scheduled. Review the checkout terms before paying; cancellation and refund arrangements must be confirmed with LORA. This website assistant cannot cancel or refund a payment.

What should I prepare, and when will my project be ready?

Send your goal, target audience, brand assets, reference links and preferred deadline. LORA will confirm feasibility, deliverables, revisions and a production schedule after reviewing the brief. Read the beginner guides →
