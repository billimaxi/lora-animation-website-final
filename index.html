01 / AI ANIMATION

From idea to animation

AI animation uses generative tools to help create images, movement or voices. Creative direction and editing turn those pieces into a coherent story.

  1. Pick one message and the audience who needs it.
  2. Write a short script and sketch a scene-by-scene storyboard.
  3. Choose a visual style; share two or three references.
  4. Review a sample before full production. Check character consistency, captions, pronunciation and brand details.

Use assets you own or have permission to use. Get consent before using someone's likeness or voice.

02 / VIDEO ADVERTISING

Make every second count

A useful ad connects a customer need to a clear offer. Animation can explain the benefit quickly, but results depend on the offer, audience and campaign.

  1. Open with a relatable problem or a strong product benefit.
  2. Show the product in action and use accurate, supportable claims.
  3. Finish with one next step: visit, message or order.
  4. Plan vertical video for mobile feeds and readable captions. Keep key text away from app controls.

Test two opening hooks and compare clicks or inquiries against your goal. Avoid promising guaranteed sales.

03 / CREATIVE BRIEFS

Give your project a head start

A brief is a shared plan, not a finished script. Send the essentials so LORA can recommend a practical scope.

  1. Describe your business, audience and desired outcome.
  2. List deliverables: video count, length, platform and format.
  3. Share your logo, brand colours, key facts and reference links.
  4. Include your budget, deadline and who will approve the work.

Example: “A 20-second vertical cake ad for local customers, ending with a WhatsApp order prompt. We have product photos and need it for next month's launch.”
